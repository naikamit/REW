Place Your Speakers
Step 1 of 2
Top-down view · All three sides of the triangle should be roughly equal length
The Golden Triangle
📏
Equal distances — speaker-to-speaker gap equals the distance from each speaker to your head If speakers are 6 ft apart, sit 6 ft from each one
↗️
Toe them in — angle each speaker slightly inward so they point toward your seat 10–20° is plenty. Both should aim at your head, not past it
👂
Tweeter at ear height — when seated, the small driver should be level with your ears Use a stand, shelf, or a stack of books to get the height right
🧱
Off the walls — pull speakers at least 2 ft from the rear wall to reduce bass buildup The further from room corners the better
Good placement does more than any cable upgrade or EQ trick. A speaker in the wrong spot creates problems no amount of tuning can fully fix. Even an approximate version of the triangle makes a huge difference.

Before You Hit Start
🪑
Sit in your sweet spot — your normal listening chair, exactly where you enjoy music We measure what your ears actually hear from that position
📱
Hold phone at ear height, mic end facing your speakers Your mic is on one of the short edges — try both and see which points more toward the speakers
🔊
Set volume to normal listening level — not whisper-quiet, not party loud Measure at the volume you actually use
🤫
Quiet the room — close doors, turn off fans, AC, and TV Background noise corrupts the measurement
💡 What you'll hear
The app plays a rising tone — low to high — through your speakers three times. It sounds unusual. That's the measurement sweep. Stay still and quiet during each 6-second pass.

Room Liveness · RT60
2 of 3
RT60 measures how long sound lingers after a note stops. Too long = muddy. Too short = unnatural. The sweet zone for a home listening room is 0.3 – 0.5 seconds.

Parametric EQ Settings
Enter these into your receiver's built-in EQ, a DSP app (Roon, JRiver, Equalizer APO), or any parametric equalizer. Cuts are always safe to apply. Boosts add energy — dial in slowly and trust your ears.
After applying EQ, re-run the measurement to confirm the changes landed correctly. Room acoustics + EQ working together is how professionals tune listening rooms.
